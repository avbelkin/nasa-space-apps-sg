# Earth Fire Calendar: video script

[Slides](demo-slides.md) · [Video script](demo-video.md) · [Project page text](project-description.md) · [Technical note](technical-note.md) · [Form fields](form-fields.md) · [Formal requirements](formal-requirements.md) · [Advice](advice.md)

A video for the **Harmonization of MODIS and VIIRS Hot Spots** challenge: a 29-second storyboard against a 30-second limit. On-screen captions and the voiceover are in English. Replace `[team name]` with the real team name before recording.

## Storyboard

| Time | Shot and action | On-screen caption | Voiceover |
|---|---|---|---|
| **0–3 s** | Globe with live points, MODIS selected | `Earth Fire Calendar` · `[team name]` · `Harmonization of MODIS and VIIRS Hot Spots` | Meet Earth Fire Calendar. |
| **3–7 s** | Same view; the **Active spots (24h)** tile is visible | `Live NASA FIRMS · last 24 h · MODIS` | MODIS and VIIRS detect fires differently. |
| **7–15 s** | Click **VIIRS**: the points become denser and the number in the **Active spots (24h)** tile grows. Only the map and the live counters are in the shot | `Live NASA FIRMS · same 24 h · VIIRS` | Same 24 hours of NASA FIRMS data: VIIRS reports far more hotspots. So their records can't simply be joined. |
| **15–23 s** | Select a location card and step through two or three months. The shot shows the **Typical / This year** tiles and the month strip, without the card text or the live counters | `Sample data` — the app's own label, which must be visible in the shot | Our calendar will show whether a month is typical for an area. Calendar values are still samples. |
| **23–29 s** | Closing shot of the globe | `Live NASA FIRMS` · `Next: harmonized archive since 2000 · validation · CSV export` and the public link to the app | Next: a harmonized, validated record for emergency managers. |

## Full voiceover

54 words, with no detection counts: the app's panel shows them.

```text
Meet Earth Fire Calendar.

MODIS and VIIRS detect fires differently.

Same 24 hours of NASA FIRMS data: VIIRS reports far more hotspots.

So their records can't simply be joined.

Our calendar will show whether a month is typical for an area.

Calendar values are still samples.

Next: a harmonized, validated record for emergency managers.
```

How to prepare the app view is in the [recording advice](advice.md#recording-the-video); what to show and what not to say is in the [breakdown of the app](advice.md#earth-fire-calendar-what-to-show-and-what-not-to).
