# Submission materials

Everything for the NASA Space Apps submission. Each folder has one purpose; put new files where the rules below say.

| Folder | What belongs there |
|---|---|
| `deliverables/` | Documents we submit or show to judges, and the data they cite. Nothing else. **Living**: keep them in step with the project at all times. |
| `presentation/` | Everything about the slide deck: slide text, slide images and numbers, sources, builds, screenshots. **Living.** |
| `video/` | Everything about the demo video: script, production plan, recording checklist, disclosures, footage. **Living.** |
| `docs/` | External reference only: the official requirements, and general advice from the official guides and our review of the 2025 winners. No information about our project. Not submitted or shown. **Frozen**: do not edit. |
| `working/` | Our own working documents about the project that are not submitted or shown: form-field plan, status against the requirements, project notes, design notes. **Living.** |
| `assets/` | Official third-party material only: logos, colours, fonts, patterns. Nothing we made. |

## Rules

- A file about the presentation goes in `presentation/`, a file about the video goes in `video/`, whatever its format. Do not put scripts, slide text or footage in `docs/` or `assets/`.
- `deliverables/` holds only what is submitted or shown, and it must change as the project develops: whenever the app, the data or the findings change, update these documents in the same piece of work.
- `docs/` is frozen and contains nothing about our project: only the official requirements and general advice, as external reference to comply with. Read it, follow it, link to it, but do not edit, add or remove files there unless the user explicitly asks. Anything that mentions Earth Fire Calendar, its app, its materials or their status belongs in a living folder. If a deliverable disagrees with `docs/`, change the deliverable.
- `working/` holds what we write about our own project for ourselves. It must stay current: update it when the app or the materials change.
- `assets/` holds only official material that is not ours. Our own screenshots, images, footage and data go next to the material that uses them.
- Do not add new top-level folders here without a reason the table above cannot cover.
