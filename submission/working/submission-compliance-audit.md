## Important distinctions

- **Permission vs attribution:** a credit is necessary where required, but cannot remove a non-commercial restriction or supply an absent redistribution grant.
- **Data vs API:** Open-Meteo's CC BY data licence and non-commercial free hosted API terms are different. Paid proprietary build/generation tools are not automatically forbidden when their permitted outputs are submitted; their binaries are not team-authored assets.
- **No LICENSE before this audit:** a clarity gap, not automatic exclusion; the frozen requirements specify an Apache-2.0 fallback. The explicit project licence now uses that same standard and does not relicense others' material.
- **Citing papers vs redistributing them:** the review's bibliography is not a copied collection of papers. Citing a non-open-access paper is not itself copyright infringement. Actual copied figures/text/software need their own permission check.
- **Real people vs fictional collaborators:** synthetic Lama is not a real under-18 participant. Only Alexey and Viktoriia count toward the team. AI tool names are factual credits, not sponsorship or official membership.
- **Team membership vs written consent:** the requirements say every person shown; no team-member exception is present. The blank [consent aid](../video/consent-template.md) records no actual consent. Keep completed records private.
- **Application start vs earlier research:** the user confirms all app work happened after the start, but research was collected/studied earlier. The strict “work on the project” wording leaves the pre-existing review's eligibility unresolved; do not infer permission from a post-start repository timestamp.
- **Draft vs submission:** seven Markdown headings, a 29-second storyboard or a local PDF is not proof of a public compliant demo. An old screenshot is not updated by changing the app's data source.
